## Transfer Rules

- If the user has direct evidence, state it clearly and specifically.
- If the user has adjacent evidence, frame it as transferable and do not overclaim.
- If the user has no evidence, mark the requirement as a gap.
- Do not claim unsupported tools, industries, metrics, companies, or credentials.

## Bullet Quality Gate

Each project or experience bullet should answer at least 2-3 of these questions:

- What was done?
- For whom, or in what product or business context?
- What problem did it solve?
- What experience, process, quality, efficiency, or collaboration improved?
- Why is it relevant to the target role?
